Movement Safety

Pregnancy exercise recommendations can vary based on individual health and pregnancy circumstances. Follow the guidance of your obstetrician, midwife, physiotherapist or other qualified healthcare professional.

Listen to your body at all times; never force a stretch or movement.

Best stretches to relieve sudden nocturnal calf cramps?

Woke up twice last night with sharp calf tightness on my left leg. Anyone else experiencing this? Any remedies that helped?

👩‍⚕️ Dr. Ananya Sharma (OB-GYN) ✓ Verified Answer

Calf cramps in Trimester 2 are very common due to altered calcium-magnesium balance. Try flexing your foot upwards (toes toward shin) rather than pointing toes. Drink plenty of warm water before bed and include a banana or soaked almonds for magnesium.
